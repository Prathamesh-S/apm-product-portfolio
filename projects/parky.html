REAL MOBILE PRODUCT

Parky

An offline-first Android parking-memory app that combines GPS, contextual clues, and simple directional guidance.

View supporting GitHub portfolio ↗

The problem

Drivers may remember the general parking area but not the floor, bay, pillar, or final approach, where GPS precision alone is insufficient.

What I owned

Independently framed the product, defined its scope, implemented core Android journeys, and tested on a physical device.

Solution and scope

Built explicit parking saves, up to six seconds of GPS sampling, visible reported accuracy, notes and photos, offline compass guidance, local history, and shortcuts.

Three-dimensional product thinking: tradeoffs

Explicit save over automatic detection; transparent accuracy over false precision; local-only storage over cloud sync; compass guidance over embedded map complexity.

Evidence and outcome

Standalone Android APK built and installed on a physical Samsung device. Core product flows and platform integrations are documented.
