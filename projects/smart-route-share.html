INDEPENDENT PRODUCT CONCEPT

Smart Route Share

Feature concept for predictable shared rides that lets the first rider set the maximum acceptable detour.

Read detailed PDF ↗

The problem

Unpredictable extra pickup stops can undermine trust in shared rides and put time-sensitive arrivals at risk.

What I owned

Independently developed a proposed rider experience, business rules, matching constraints, and MVP requirements.

Solution and scope

Proposed opt-in sharing after Rider A’s verified pickup, constrained matching for Rider B, bilateral consent, and fare discounts only after B actually boards.

Three-dimensional product thinking: tradeoffs

Detour control over maximizing occupancy; single second rider in MVP; no discount based solely on a match; original fare preserved on no-show.

Evidence and outcome

Delivered a proposed product flow, testable requirements, edge cases, and an MVP success-metrics framework.

Evidence boundary

Concept case study only. Not shipped, not validated with riders, and no user- or revenue-impact results are claimed.
