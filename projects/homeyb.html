REAL MOBILE PRODUCT

HomeyB

A privacy-aware hyperlocal Android app for nearby discovery, local questions, topic-based interactions, and consent-based connections.

View supporting GitHub portfolio ↗

The problem

People seeking immediate local information often lack access to nearby, contextually relevant contacts without joining established communities.

What I owned

Led product framing, requirements, MVP scope, privacy decisions, and technical implementation, collaborating with a co-founder on feedback, testing, and release coordination.

Solution and scope

Implemented approximate-location discovery, Live Buzz, Interest Hives, structured Highlights, Activity, mutual connections, and safety features.

Three-dimensional product thinking: tradeoffs

Approximate H3 cells rather than public coordinates; foreground presence instead of background tracking; mutual consent before direct messages; expiry for local conversations.

Evidence and outcome

A working React Native / Expo / Firebase Android prototype with controlled two-device demonstrations. Investigated an ambiguous location-verification publishing failure using hypothesis-driven debugging.

Product screenshots

Controlled test-build images; not evidence of customer adoption.

Evidence boundary

The repository does not establish statistically meaningful retention or public adoption. Testing-track status needs independent Play Console confirmation. Screenshots and demo use test data.
