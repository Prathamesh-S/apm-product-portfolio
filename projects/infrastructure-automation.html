REAL-WORLD IMPACT

Infrastructure Reporting Automation

Reduced recurring infrastructure reporting preparation from approximately 30–60 minutes to ~5 minutes per run.

Read detailed PDF ↗

The problem

Manual server and database checks required switching between hosts, collecting health indicators, and preparing multiple reports as often as 3–4 times daily.

What I owned

Independently identified and solved a repeated internal reporting burden for a technical team supporting a banking client.

Solution and scope

Built an on-demand Linux shell-script workflow that collects metrics and generates timestamped, browser-readable HTML summaries. The team used it regularly while I was employed.

Three-dimensional product thinking: tradeoffs

On-demand execution over scheduling; reusable HTML output over manual consolidation; reporting snapshot rather than duplicating existing diagnostic logs.

Evidence and outcome

Preparation time fell from an estimated 30–60 minutes to about five minutes per run. All roughly ten team members could access the workflow, though individual usage was not measured.

Evidence boundary

This was a manually triggered automation, not an unattended monitoring system. Impact estimates are based on observed workflow timing, not an audited study.
