What I bring to a product team

Technical execution with product judgment.

I spot workflow problems worth fixing.

On a team supporting enterprise infrastructure, I recognized repeated manual reporting and independently developed a more efficient workflow, then saw the team use it during my tenure.

I can move from requirements to a working product.

HomeyB and Parky required MVP scoping, user journeys, implementation decisions, Android integrations and device-level validation—not just feature ideas.

I make tradeoffs explicit.

Parky favors transparent GPS accuracy and local storage; HomeyB uses approximate location, foreground presence and consent-based messaging. Each simplifies or protects a different aspect of user experience.

I investigate before prescribing fixes.

When HomeyB surfaced an ambiguous location-related publishing failure, I examined the flow and error mapping rather than assuming the first hypothesis was correct. My analytical studies similarly distinguish metric movement from business value.
